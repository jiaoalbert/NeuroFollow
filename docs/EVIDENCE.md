# NeuroFollow evidence and pathway audit

## Provenance and limits

The user supplied Moonis et al., **Management of Incidentally Discovered Pineal Cyst on CT and MRI: Recommendations from the ACR Incidental Findings Committee**, JACR 2026;23:117–122, DOI [10.1016/j.jacr.2025.09.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacr.2025.09.006). Its text and Figures 1–2 were inspected directly. The PDF is not redistributed in this repository.

Other references identify published radiology reviews, society guidelines, or consensus statements. Independent retrieval from PMC, AJNR, DOI.org, Europe PMC, and Crossref was blocked by the environment's network policy. Their full text, link resolution, and currency have **not** been reverified in this revision. Do not represent this as a systematic review or a complete implementation of all society algorithms. Newer pituitary/cavernous guidance in particular requires editorial review before clinical deployment. `sources` records this distinction in the UI.

## Pineal algorithm transcription

Applies to adults ≥18 years with an incidental finding and no attributable symptoms or obstructive features. Radiologist/referrer discretion applies to limited life expectancy or major comorbidities. All dates below are **after the initial study**, unless stated otherwise.

| Modality | Cyst      | Diameter | Initial action             | Stable first follow-up                                                                  | Stable second follow-up      |
| -------- | --------- | -------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| MRI      | Simple    | <15 mm   | No further imaging         | —                                                                                       | —                            |
| MRI      | Simple    | ≥15 mm   | MRI at 6–12 months         | MRI at 18–24 months from initial study                                                  | No further imaging           |
| MRI      | Nonsimple | <10 mm   | MRI at 6 months            | MRI at 18 months from initial study                                                     | No further imaging           |
| MRI      | Nonsimple | ≥10 mm   | Neurosurgical consultation | Specialist-directed                                                                     | Specialist-directed          |
| CT       | Simple    | <15 mm   | No further imaging         | —                                                                                       | —                            |
| CT       | Simple    | ≥15 mm   | MRI at 6 months            | Figure 2 footnote 5: transition to second MRI follow-up at 18–24 months from initial CT | No further imaging if stable |
| CT       | Nonsimple | <10 mm   | No further imaging         | —                                                                                       | —                            |
| CT       | Nonsimple | ≥10 mm   | Neurosurgical consultation | Specialist-directed                                                                     | Specialist-directed          |

Overrides at any stage: increased size/complexity during surveillance, aqueduct compromise with or without hydrocephalus, tectal signal abnormality/hypoattenuation, or attributable symptoms → neurosurgical consultation. Acute deterioration → urgent clinical assessment. Isolated headache alone without raised-pressure/mass-effect features is insufficient for neurosurgical consultation. Simple = thin/imperceptible wall and uniform fluid signal/attenuation. Nonsimple = thick wall, nodular component, or nonsimple fluid. This does not mean every nonsimple cyst is malignant.

## Other pathways

| Finding               | Decision inputs                                                                                                                    | Evidence and limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pituitary             | Size, solid vs cystic, optic apparatus, visual deficit, endocrine findings, growth                                                 | ACR 2018 white paper plus Endocrine Society 2011. Surveillance intervals explicitly follow the latter. No unverified ACR sub-5-mm or cystic algorithm is implemented. Prolactinoma management is often medical.                                                                                                                 |
| Arachnoid             | CSF signal/diffusion, size, CSF-flow-sensitive location, hydrocephalus, symptoms, growth                                           | Natural-history review plus radiology review. <2.5 cm is a small-cyst descriptor, not a surgical threshold.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Colloid               | Age <65, headache, diameter ≥7 mm, FLAIR hyperintensity, third-ventricular risk zone, hydrocephalus                                | Jenkinson review. Five-item CCRS is shown only when all inputs are known; ≤2 lower, 3 intermediate, ≥4 higher risk. The score supports discussion, not automatic surgery. Annual clinical review for 2–3 years is pragmatic, not a validated MRI schedule.                                                                      |
| Meningioma            | Edema, critical structures, symptoms, growth, suspected/grade 1 vs other                                                           | EANO 2021 and radiology review. Annual MRI for 5 years then generally every 2 years applies to suspected/WHO grade 1 observation, not higher grades.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Vestibular schwannoma | Extrameatal size, brainstem relationship, hearing/audiometry, growth                                                               | EANO 2020 and radiology review. Annual MRI/audiometry for 5 years for untreated observed tumors. Large compressive tumors need surgical assessment; size alone does not establish compression.                                                                                                                                  |
| White matter          | Vascular vs demyelinating/atypical pattern, clinical episodes, risks, change                                                       | WMH consensus 2023 and radiology review. No antiplatelet solely for incidental WMH. No invented routine MRI interval.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Aneurysm              | Age, fitness/preferences, size, arterial location, extradural vs intradural, morphology, growth, symptoms, prior SAH, risk factors | AHA/ASA 2015, ESO 2022, MRA radiology review. ≥7 mm is an editorial risk-discussion flag, **not** a guideline treatment cutoff. Size bands provide context; no PHASES/UIATS score is computed. Preventive-treatment risk must be compared with rupture risk.                                                                    |
| Carotid               | NASCET-equivalent percent, recent ipsilateral ischemia, measurement certainty, eligibility, high-risk features                     | ESVS 2023 and MRA review. Symptomatic 70–99% versus 50–69% versus <50% have different CEA considerations. Selected asymptomatic 60–99% require additional criteria. Annual duplex is restricted to selected 50–60% asymptomatic potential intervention candidates. Complete occlusion and near-occlusion are separate branches. |
| DVA                   | Isolated vs associated cavernoma vs venous complication, symptoms                                                                  | AJNR 2023 radiology review. Preserve DVA drainage. Associated lesions require their own pathway.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Cavernous             | Location, symptomatic bleed, seizure, multiplicity/family history, susceptibility MRI, change                                      | Angioma Alliance 2017 plus radiology review; older guidance, not claimed current. No fixed surveillance or diameter-only treatment threshold.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Skull                 | Confident benign vs indeterminate/aggressive, cancer history, pain, CT/MRI, growth                                                 | Radiological skull review 2018. Imaging characterization, not a universal management algorithm or extrapolated long-bone Bone-RADS schedule.                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Implementation safeguards

- No preselected normal imaging or clinical answers.
- Required fields and units/ranges are checked in both the form and rule function.
- Unknown decision-critical information does not yield reassurance.
- Acute red flags override missing inputs in the rule function. The form explicitly alerts on acute red flags without requiring the remaining answers.
- Pediatric cases are directed away from adult pathways.
- Results separate imaging, clinical considerations, rationale, and source links.
- Editing an answer invalidates the previous result. Inputs and results survive tab navigation but are not persisted to disk or localStorage.
- Tests cover source coverage, distinct schemas, missing/invalid inputs, thresholds, overrides, referral conditions, and unsupported interval avoidance. They validate implementation consistency, **not clinical accuracy**.
